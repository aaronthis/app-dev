## Gaiyou
==***Shingeki no Kyojin***== The story is set in a world where humanity is forced to live within three concentric massive stone walls—Wall Maria, Wall Rose, and Wall Sina to protect themselves from Titans, giant mindless humanoids that devour humans for no apparent reason. For 100 years, peace prevailed, until a **"Colossal Titan"** appears out of nowhere and breaches the outermost wall. The story follows Eren Yeager, his adoptive ==sister/~lover~== **Mikasa Ackerman**, and their friend **Armin Arlert**, who joined the military's elite **Survey Corps** to reclaim their world and seek revenge after a tragic loss.

### Shujinko

-**Eren Yeager** 
: A fiercely determined youth who vows to eradicate every Titan after witnessing his mother’s death, eventually evolving into a complex figure driven by an uncompromising quest for freedom.

-**Mikasa Ackerman** 
: An incredibly skilled and stoic soldier who ranks as the top of her class, motivated primarily by a deep, protective devotion to her only remaining family, Eren.

-**Armin Arlert** 
: While physically weaker than his peers, he is a brilliant strategist whose tactical genius and hope for a world beyond the walls often prove more decisive than raw strength.
